## Writing rules

The English text is reference text for readers new to the series: neutral, plain, specific.

- Translate the meaning, not word by word. Keep every fact in the Japanese; add none.
- No em dashes or en dashes. Use commas, periods, colons, or parentheses.
- No "not X but Y" framing unless both halves carry a fact.
- No one-line closers that restate the point ("This changed everything.").
- No inflated significance: avoid pivotal, crucial, key (adjective), testament, legacy, underscores, highlights, showcases, delve, intricate, vibrant, landscape.
- Prefer "is", "has", "was" over "serves as", "stands as", "boasts".
- No forced lists of three. Vary sentence length.
- Straight quotes ("..."), not curly quotes.
- Keep uncertainty that the Japanese states ("is said to", "has not been revealed"). Do not add new hedges.
